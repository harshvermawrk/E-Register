#  Management System as E-Register

## What is this?

The ** Management System** is a web-based management application designed to maintain and organize the financial and member-related records of our yearly  festival.

The main purpose is to replace the traditional register-based approach with a digital system where important information can be stored, searched, updated, and managed in one place.

This project is being developed initially as an **internal application** for our own use. Depending on the requirements and future development, access may later be provided to other people.

## Why am I building this?

Managing a yearly festival involves keeping track of many things:

- Member information
- Yearly contributions
- Credit and debit transactions
- Festival expenses
- Payments and pending amounts
- Historical records
- Other important information

Keeping all of this in physical registers can make searching, updating, and maintaining historical records difficult.

This project aims to make that process simpler and more organized.

## Main Goals

- Digitize the existing record-keeping process.
- Maintain yearly financial records.
- Store member information.
- Track income and expenses.
- Keep credit and debit records.
- Make information easier to search and manage.
- Maintain historical records for future reference.
- Reduce dependency on physical registers.
